# Web Express Terms of Service

These terms are the binding rules and guidelines for using the University of Colorado Boulder Web Express service. Agreement to these terms is required for access to creating and maintaining websites on the platform.

## Key Takeaways
* **Secure, University-Managed Platform:** CU Boulder hosts and maintains Web Express, handling security updates, system monitoring, and emergency backups. Only users with a campus IdentiKey can access site editing tools.
* **Prohibited Data:** Highly Confidential Data (like student records or health data) is strictly prohibited from being collected or posted.
* **No Technical Skills Required:** The platform allows the community to create professional sites without coding. Users cannot install custom software or themes to ensure security and brand consistency.
* **Support and Training:** Strategic Relations and Communications (SRC) provides hosting, setup, documentation, training, and regular feature updates.
* **Analytics:** Sites can use Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager. Site owners must manage their own accounts and ensure no personal information is collected.
* **Policy Compliance:** Sites must follow accessibility, branding, copyright, and acceptable-use policies. Abandoned or non-compliant sites may be archived or removed.

---

## Service Description
Web Express is the University of Colorado Boulder’s web **Content Management System (CMS)**, a Foundational Service powered by **Drupal**. It is designed for units to achieve content and marketing goals without extensive programming knowledge.

### Usage Restrictions
* **No Code Access:** Access to administrative settings, configurations, modules, or core code is prohibited.
* **No Customizations:** Users cannot add custom modules, themes, or code updates.
* **Intended Use:** Web Express is for public-facing sites (Colleges, Schools, Departments, Faculty, Student Groups, etc.).
* **Prohibited Use:** It should **not** be used for Intranets, private workspaces, internal collaboration, course websites, or personal websites.

---

## Service Maintenance and Support
Web Express uses a **continuous improvement and continuous development (CI/CD)** process. The university reserves the right to access logs and site content to resolve system problems.

### Backups
Backups are performed regularly **solely for emergency response or disaster recovery.**

### Support Resources
* **Bugs:** Issues should be reported to [websupport@colorado.edu](mailto:websupport@colorado.edu).
* **Browser Support:** The team supports modern browsers in coordination with [OIT standards](https://oit.colorado.edu/software-hardware/software-lifecycle#webbrowsers).
* **Feature Requests:** Submit ideas via the [WebCentral Feature Request page](https://www.colorado.edu/webcentral/feature-request).

---

## Data Retention
In accordance with **APS2006**, site owners must regularly review content for accuracy.
* **Manual Cleanup:** Owners should delete out-of-date information and download records needed for long-term reference.
* **Not an Archive:** The platform is not approved for long-term record-keeping or retaining private information.

---

## Google Analytics 4 (GA4) & Tag Manager
Web Express integrates with **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** and **Google Tag Manager (GTM)**.

| Requirement | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Governance** | Owners are responsible for data accuracy, retention, and user access. |
| **Admin Access** | It is highly recommended to add `websupport@colorado.edu` as an admin to prevent lockouts during staff turnover. |
| **Data Retention** | GA4 defaults to 2 months; it is recommended to update this to **14 months**. |
| **PII Review** | Owners must ensure no personally identifiable information (PII) is collected. |

## Technical Specifications
* **Service Outages:** SRC coordinates with campus partners to resolve downtime as quickly as possible.
* **File Limits:** The environment is not a media server. Individual files are limited to **100MB**.
* **Site Access:** Access is granted only via **IdentiKey**. Service or shared accounts are strictly prohibited.

---

## Service Use & Responsible Use
By using Web Express, you agree to comply with:
* Web Development Guidelines
* Acceptable Use of CU Boulder’s IT Resources
* eCommunications Policy
* APS 6010: Data Governance

> **Important:** No Web Express website may receive, store, transmit, or process credit card information.

### Accessibility
All websites must be accessible to people with disabilities in compliance with **Section 504**, the **ADA**, and **Colorado’s Digital Accessibility Law**.

### Content Responsibility
* **Subject Matter Expertise:** Do not publish content outside your area of expertise.
* **Moderation:** Owners must monitor and remove pornographic, illegal, or spam content.
* **Copyright:** Unauthorized use of copyrighted materials is prohibited. Content must be original, licensed, or public domain.

### Private Information
Sites must **not** contain or collect:
* [Highly Confidential Data](https://www.cu.edu/data-governance/resources-support/data-classification)
* FERPA protected data (Student records)
* HIPAA protected data (Health info)
* CUI, FCI, or NCAA regulated data

---

## User Management
* **Invitations:** Users are invited via `identikey@colorado.edu`.
* **Offboarding:** Access must be removed promptly when a business need no longer exists.
* **Outside Designers:** Must be provisioned as a **CU Boulder POI** and are bound by these same Terms of Service.

---

## URLs and Publishing
* **Domain:** Sites must use a `colorado.edu` URL. Redirecting to external domains is prohibited.
* **Sandbox:** New sites begin at a sandbox URL before launching.

### Site Removal & Archival
SRC may remove content or entire sites for policy violations (Security, Copyright, Brand, etc.). A site is considered **abandoned** if:
1.  It has not been edited in more than **12 months**.
2.  The owner is no longer employed by CU Boulder.

**Archival Process:**
* Owners are contacted 30 days before archival.
* Archived sites are **permanently deleted after 90 days** and cannot be recovered.

## Changes to Terms
The Terms of Service may be modified at any time. Immediate action (deactivation or removal) may be taken without notice if system security or operations are at risk.
